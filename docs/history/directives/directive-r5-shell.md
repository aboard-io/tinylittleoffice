Paul (2026-10-10), verbatim:
"Unless documents are hard constrained in their width and height by their form, like decks, we shouldn't really have viewports—take over the whole screen with no border. At least on mobile. On desktop just keep text within margins."
"There's a regression. It used to be as docs grew vertically and I scrolled down the main nav would scroll up out of the way, the hamburger would push left. And the secondary nav would fix itself at the top. This was a smooth animation."
"The title is too small to edit. When I tap it, open a wide editing interface."

Build, three parts, one branch:

1. No viewports. The document is the page, not a card on it. On a phone (max-width 640px or coarse pointer): `.to-main` has no side gutters around the document; every app's outer frame loses its border, radius and shadow and runs edge to edge (the Word Processor's paper colour runs the full width, its words keep an inset of 16px; the Spreadsheet's grid starts at the left edge with its row numbers; the Outliner, To-Do List, Kanban Board, Calendar, Calculator, Trip Planner's map, Website Builder's frame, Drawing Tool, Diagram Maker: the same). On desktop the frames go too (no border, no shadow, no card), and what is text keeps its measure: the Word Processor's words within the paper's margins at its page width (the paper colour may run full width, the words stay at 816/794), the Outliner and To-Do within their current max-width, the Spreadsheet, boards, canvases and maps the full width of the main column. Hard-constrained forms keep their frame: the Presentation Builder's slide (16:9), the Animator's stage, the pixel grid; the chart in a sheet keeps its box. Dark mode: the paper tokens still read as paper. Nothing inside the editors changes; this is CSS plus any small structural change the CSS needs. Check every app at 390px (WebKit) and at 1280px with screenshots in the report.

2. The header scrolls away. The header (`.to-header`) is no longer sticky: it scrolls off with the page. The menu button (the Office mark) is `position: fixed` at the top left (top accounting for `--to-vv-top`, the visual viewport's offset when a phone's keyboard pushes the page), so it is always reachable; when the header has scrolled past, it sits on a small card of its own (surface, 8px radius, a soft shadow) over whatever is under it. The app's bar (`to-bar`, the first in the editor) is sticky at the top of the viewport (`top: var(--to-vv-top, 0px)`), with an opaque full-width strip behind it (nothing of the page shows beside it); once the header is gone it makes room for the menu button on its left (margin-left, transition 0.2s ease, "the hamburger would push left" and the bar slides over), narrowed rather than padded so a bar that scrolls sideways on a phone never slides under the button. A `scrolled` attribute on `to-shell` toggled in a requestAnimationFrame from window scroll, visualViewport scroll and resize: true when the header's bottom is at or above the viewport's top. The arrival banner (short-link expiry) keeps working: sticky under the bar or small, as it is now, check it. On the home page (no bar) the header simply scrolls. Reduced motion: no transition.

3. The title is edited wide. The header's title (`input.to-title`) stays where it is, small, until it is tapped or focused. Then, in place (no dialog, no popover; Paul's rule: words are edited where they are drawn), the header opens a second row: the title field spans the header's full width at 20px, 44px tall, with its placeholder visible, and the first row keeps the mark, the app icon, the trust state, the meter, Share and Download. Enter or Escape or a tap elsewhere closes the row (Escape restores the title it had). The field's text is selected on open when the title is the automatic one ("New Spreadsheet 2026-10-10 #1"), so typing replaces it. A smooth height transition (0.2s, none under reduced motion). On a phone the same, with the 16px rule (the field is never under 16px). While the title is being edited the header does not scroll away (it is at the top anyway: scroll to top on open if needed). Write the title rule and the scrolling rule into docs/editing.md §1 in two sentences.
