# R7-pinch: a pinch is the size of everything, and the words reflow

Read first: directive-r7-common.md, src/kit/zoom.ts in full (size(), applySize(), stepSize(), zoomed(), the root's CSS `zoom`), src/kit/drawer.ts (the "Size of everything" row), src/styles/kit.css (html/body rules at the top, `to-bar`'s sticky rule ~line 90, the `[scrolled]` rules ~1190–1235, `.to-size` ~2222), src/kit/shell.ts (onViewport/measure: `--to-vv-top`, the pinched-in branch), the six files that divide by zoomed() (kit/menu.ts place, context.ts, link-pop.ts, complete.ts, find.ts), src/apps/draw/editor.ts's pinch (~197–210, 418–436, 498–500, 560) as the one that exists, test/browser/languages.test.ts (the 390px drawer and bar checks) and docs/editing.md.

Build:
1. **The browser's pinch is off on touch screens, ours is on.** `touch-action: pan-x pan-y` on `html` (iOS 13+ and Android honour it; add `pinch-zoom` nowhere). A new `src/kit/pinch.ts`: one listener set on the document (pointer events with two active pointers, `touch` type only; `gesturestart/gesturechange/gestureend` as WebKit's fallback where two pointers never arrive) that reports a gesture's scale and its midpoint, and the kit's own use of it: unless the gesture began inside a `[data-spatial]` element (the spatial canvases of R7-spatial, which take their own), the size of everything follows the pinch live (size × scale, clamped to SIZE_MIN…SIZE_MAX, applied each frame through applySize without the store), the point under the fingers stays under them (before the change note the element and the offset under the midpoint, after it scroll so it is back there — words reflow, so an element, not a pixel), and at the gesture's end the size is kept (store) rounded to the nearest 5%. A one-finger drag during the pinch is the browser's pan as ever. The export is small: `onPinch(el, { start, move, end })` for the canvases too, so R7-spatial uses the same reading of the fingers as the kit.
2. **SIZE_MAX = 2.5, NAV_MAX = 1.5.** The stepper reads the same; `stepSize` keeps its 10% steps from wherever a pinch left it (round to the step first). The nav stops at 150%: zoom.ts exports `navSize()` = min(size, NAV_MAX), and applySize sets, beside the root's `zoom`, a root property `--to-nav-zoom` = navSize/size, which the kit's CSS applies as `zoom` to the nav: `to-bar`, `.to-header` (burger and title), the drawer, `.to-dialog` and the document dialogs, the context bar (`<to-context>`), menus, the link and note boxes, the completion list, the find bar, the pills; every one of them in one rule list in kit.css under one comment, so an app's own bar (the Animator's sliders, the Drawing Tool's tool rail: anything that is nav, not document) joins the list rather than inventing its own. Everything placed from page coordinates divides by its own effective zoom (zoomed() × the nav zoom where the placed thing is nav): fix each of the six. The document (the editor's words, cards, pictures, the home page's rows) keeps the root's size. While a pinch is in hand a pill shows the percentage (the drawer's own words, "140%"), top centre, above everything, gone half a second after the fingers lift; nothing else in the UI moves for it.
3. **Everything fits at every size.** At 390×844 and the size at 2.5: the bar is one row at 150% that scrolls sideways under the burger's card (no second row, no label wrapped), the burger is where the finger expects it, the drawer opens at 150% and its rows fit (the size row wraps as it already may), the Share dialog and the templates dialog fit the screen at 150% with their buttons reachable, the Word Processor's words are at 250% and reflow to the width (no horizontal scroll of the page: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth`), a table card scrolls inside itself, the Outliner's rows reflow, a Kanban column is still a column. Whatever breaks at 2.5 is fixed in the kit's CSS, not papered over per app. The shell's `--to-vv-top` keeps following the visual viewport for the keyboard, and the pinched-in branch in measure() stays (the system zoom still pinches).
4. **Popovers under zoom.** Everything placed from page coordinates already divides by zoomed(); check each of the six at 2.5 with a test or by hand and fix what lands wrong (the context bar over a selection, the link box, the completion list, the menu).
5. **docs/editing.md** gains the "Pinch and size" section from the common directive's five points, in the standard's voice.

Tests (browser, 390×844, failing before): two synthetic touch pointers spreading over the Word Processor change `size()` live and the pill shows; lifting keeps it rounded; at size 2.5 the bar's height and the burger's box are those of 1.5 (measured rects) while a paragraph's font size is 2.5 times its normal; the same gesture begun inside a `[data-spatial]` element changes nothing; a pinch keeps the paragraph under the fingers in view (its top within the viewport after); at size 2.5 the fit assertions of point 3 across the home page, Write, Outline and Board; stepSize after a pinch moves in tens.
