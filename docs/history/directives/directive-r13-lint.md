# R13-lint — two findings more, and a pinch in the show

Two small things Paul's use of 2026-10-11 turned up, after round twelve's lint.

## 1. A formula that fails is a finding

Paul: *"Your total in the spreadsheet is flagged CIRC."* A `=SUM(B2:B8)` in a sheet fence's eighth data row took in its own cell (rows count from the first data row, not the header), and the lint said nothing: it does not evaluate. Add to `src/lint.ts` (through the Spreadsheet's own lint, `src/apps/sheet/lint.ts`, and run on every table block in any document, a fence's included): a cell whose formula evaluates to an error (`#CIRC`, `#REF`, `#DIV/0`, `#NAME` and the rest the engine has, `src/doc/formula.ts`) is a finding naming the table, the cell (`B8`), the formula, the error and the fix (for `#CIRC`: "the range includes the cell itself; rows count from the first data row: `=SUM(B1:B7)`"). A node test with the codebase document's own sheet as the fixture (`test/fixtures/lint/sheet-circ.tlo`: the table above with the wrong range) and a clean one. The CLI's `lint` and `measure` show it; the skill's "Check it, do not look at it" list gains the line.

## 2. Two fingers in a deck's show

Paul: *"I should be able to pinch zoom where appropriate in view modes too."* The reader and the embed zoom their pages in place since 08d3e0f (`src/kit/shell.ts` pinchZoom over `.to-display-pages`). The show (`src/apps/deck/editor.ts` `.deck-show`, presenting) does not: add the same, with `pinchZoom` from `src/kit/pinch.ts`: the frame is `.deck-show` (marked `data-spatial`), the picture the slide's `.frame`, the zoom a CSS variable the frame's `width` multiplies (1 to 4, kept in quarters when the fingers lift), the slide panned by scrolling the show (which becomes a scroller when the slide is larger than it). Arrow keys and taps still turn slides; a pinch never does. A browser test with the pinch helper of `test/browser/pinch.test.ts` (import it or lift it into `test/browser/helpers.ts`): a pinch over the slide grows it in place and leaves the size of everything alone; a tap after it still goes to the next slide.
