# R9-chrome: the bar glides, the header breathes, the hamburger keeps out of the way, the drawer is tidy

Read first: directive-r9-common.md, directive-r8-shell.md (what the chrome is now), src/kit/chrome.ts in full (the column, the header, the burger, the bar's place on a phone, the hiding while a box has focus), src/kit/drawer.ts (the head with the language picker and Close, the "This document" section round eight added with Share… and Download, the apps), src/kit/share-dialog.ts (how Share opens from the drawer), src/kit/exports.ts (the Download table, opened from the drawer), src/styles/kit.css (the chrome and drawer rules) and src/styles/responsive.css, test/browser/chrome.test.ts, drawer.test.ts, download.test.ts, shell-scroll.test.ts, pinch.test.ts (what asserts the header's buttons and the burger today), docs/editing.md's phone paragraph.

Paul, 2026-10-10, from his iPhone, five things:
1. "Could you animate the bottom bar? When the keyboard appears and disappears it jumps and it's overwhelming. Could move briskly or fade in and fade out."
2. "Let's get rid of share and download in the top nav and move that functionality to the hamburger — it's already there. Now the title has room to breathe."
3. "Make the hamburger translucent when I'm editing. Maybe even move it further top right animating it so that it doesn't bump text or content I'm editing."
4. "The hamburger design is janky": the drawer's "This document" section draws its heading at the left and Share… and Download as two boxes stacked at the right with empty space under the heading.
5. "It has a border that has a gap between border and the main menu area": a thick vertical line stands apart from the drawer's left edge. It is the focus ring of the `<dialog>` itself, which takes the focus on a touch screen since the search field must not (the keyboard), drawn outside the panel's border.

Build:
1. **The bar glides.** The column's height follows the visual viewport through a transition of about 200 ms, ease-out, so the bar moves to its place above the keyboard and back; while the height changes the bar fades (opacity, about 120 ms out and in) so no one reads it mid-flight, back when the column settles (transitionend, a timer as the fallback). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the height changes at once and only the fade remains. keepCaretVisible runs after the settle, not during.
2. **The header is the title.** On a phone and on a desktop alike, Share and Download leave the header; the drawer's "This document" section holds them (it does already) and the keys (Mod+S for Share, Mod+Shift+D for Download if the standard has them; else as docs/editing.md says) still work. What stays in the header: the app's icon (new document), the title, the trust pill, the meter, and the burger. The title field takes the room that is freed. The Share dialog's and the Download table's own tests open them from the drawer now (or by key), never from a header button.
3. **The hamburger keeps out of the way.** While a box has focus on a phone (the header hidden), the burger sits in the very corner (an inset of about 0.25em from the top and the right, within the safe area) rather than where the header had it, and gets there with a transition of about 200 ms (transform or inset, not a jump); it is translucent (opacity about 0.55, opaque on hover, on focus-visible and while the drawer is open) so the words under it show through; it keeps its 44px target. When the header returns, it slides back to its place in the header row. Under reduced motion: no slide.
4. **The drawer's document section** is a list like the apps': a small heading ("This document"), then Share… and Download as full-width rows (icon, label, the keys at the right), the Download row opening the table in place or as its menu (whichever the table is today); no boxes, no empty column. It appears only with a document open (as now) and sits above the search, so the order is: head (language, Close), This document, search, apps, the rest.
5. **No ring, no gap.** The drawer's `<dialog>` holds focus on a touch screen without drawing a focus ring (`outline: none` on the dialog itself is right: it is not a control; the first row gets `:focus-visible` as ever when the keys move), and its edge is one hairline against the backdrop, the panel flush with it.
6. docs/editing.md's phone paragraph says where Share and Download live and how the burger behaves while editing.

Tests (browser, 390×844 with a coarse pointer, and 1440×900; failing before): a faked visual viewport 300px shorter sets a transition on the column and the bar's opacity dips to 0 and returns; reduced motion emulated gives the target height at once; the header holds no Share or Download button and the title field is wider than before by at least their width; focus in the words moves the burger to within 0.5em of the top-right corner with opacity under 0.7, and a tap outside returns it; the drawer's document section is two full-width rows under its heading, the heading above the first row and both rows the drawer's width less the gutters; the dialog's computed outline-style is none when focused; Share and Download still open from the drawer and by their keys (download.test.ts and the share tests updated to open that way).
