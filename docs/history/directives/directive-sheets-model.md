2. **Locked cells.** A sheet may say which cells a reader should not type over (the model's formulas) so a template is safe to play with: a header `lock: B6:D9, F2` (ranges), shown with a subtle lock look in the editor, skipped by typing/paste/fill, and with an "Unlock" affordance in the column/row menu or the bar for the author.
