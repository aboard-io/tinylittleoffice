# R15-embeds — a thing from the web on a slide is the thing, not its address

**Paul, 2026-10-11**, on a deck of the Bronx's history with pictures from Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia cards and YouTube films: *"The markdown images show as markdown with line breaks, other images don't show and YouTube links are URLs with nothing else."* He was right and the lint was wrong: the lint said each slide "shows a picture" or "shows the thing from the web"; what the slide showed was a broken-image glyph and a white card reading "Video eRVYwDtI2E8 · YouTube · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eRVYwDtI2E8".

## What is wrong today

1. **A web address alone on a slide** (`src/apps/deck/view.ts` subPage, the last branch) is drawn as a card of the provider's name and the raw address, in the SVG. In the editor and in the show, that is all a reader ever sees: no title, no picture, nothing to tap. The Word Processor does this right: its `<to-embed>` (kit/embeds.ts) is a card with the provider and a way to load, and the thing itself once tapped, nothing asked of the provider until then.
2. **A picture on a slide** (`![alt](url)`, subPage's `img` branch) is an SVG `<image>` of the address. Gated and proxied by kit/pictures.ts gateSvg, it shows for a trusted host only once the picture proxy answers (on the dev box, `npm run media:dev` on 8788 must be running; on production the media Worker is); a redirecting address (Commons' `Special:FilePath`) comes back as a broken glyph, and so does any address the proxy cannot fetch. The reader sees a broken picture, not a card saying what it is.
3. **Somewhere a picture's markdown shows as text with line breaks** (Paul, on a phone). Find it: the thumbnail rail, the words box when a slide holding a picture is edited, the speaker notes, the share card, the reader at phone width. Reproduce it in a test and fix it.

## The design

- **In the editor and in the show, a thing from the web is the live card**: the deck's slide (`src/apps/deck/editor.ts`, the slide's HTML over the SVG where the sub-document is) draws `<to-embed url=…>` in the sub-document's box, the same card as the Word Processor's, with the provider, the author's title and a tap that loads the frame (YouTube's privacy endpoint, Wikipedia's summary, the picture through the proxy). Presenting, the card is tappable too; arrow keys still turn slides. Nothing is asked of a provider until the tap, as the kit's rule says.
- **The author's title**: a markdown link alone on a line, `[The neighbourhood the road replaced](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…)`, is a thing from the web with a title (kernel: `isEmbed` in src/doc/model.ts accepts a paragraph that is one link run whose href is a web address; the writer keeps it as written; the grammar in test/doc/tlo-grammar.ts says so and the properties still hold). `embedCaption` gives the title the author wrote, else the provider's best ("YouTube video" rather than "Video eRVYwDtI2E8": no id in a title). A bare address stays as it is.
- **In the SVG page** (the reader, the export, the thumbnail), a thing from the web is a card drawn by its kind: a play glyph on a dark field for a film or a song, the article glyph for a Wikipedia card, the provider's name small, the author's title large, the address not drawn at all (an export's PDF carries it as a link: the card is an `<a href>` in the SVG, so a tap in the reader opens the thing on its provider in a new tab). For a picture, the picture, through the proxy, and where it cannot be fetched a card with the alt text and the picture glyph, never the browser's broken glyph: the proxy's failure is answered by `onerror` in the live page and by the card in the export.
- **The lint tells the truth**: `shows: 'web'` is said only when the kernel reads an embed; a picture's address that is not direct (a `Special:FilePath`, a page rather than a file) is a finding with the fix ("Commons: use the file's upload.wikimedia.org address; the link page redirects and the proxy does not follow it"), and the measure reports, per slide, the embed's kind, provider and title.
- **The dev box**: `docs/translation.md`'s sibling for agents, the skill's "Check it" section, says the picture proxy must run for pictures to show on a dev site (`npm run media:dev` with SITE), as the shortener must.

- **Links in a slide's words are links** (Paul, 2026-10-11: *"I also need to be able to click on links in an offered deck"*): in the SVG page (the reader, the export) each link run is an `<a href>` with the words underlined in the theme's link colour, and in the editor and the show a tap on one follows it through the link guard (kit/link-guard.ts: the site it goes to shown large, Cancel the default), as a link in the Word Processor's reader does; the arrow keys and a tap elsewhere still turn slides. A browser test: a slide with a Wikipedia link, tapped in the reader and in the show, opens the guard naming en.wikipedia.org.
