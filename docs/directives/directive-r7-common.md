# Round seven — the common contract

Everything in directive-r4-common.md holds (read it first: the tree, the standing rules, pacing on a 2-core box, the report, the node_modules rule), and directive-r6-common.md's two rules (fix the mechanism; every host gets it). This round is one idea of Paul's, 2026-10-10, seen on an iPhone after a pinch on the Word Processor left the bar four times its size over an empty page:

> "If I'm a low vision user I want big scrollable nav and a useful hamburger and then I want the element to be rendered as large and legible as possible. For a word processor I'd expect the text to be reflowed in the boundaries of the fixed viewport. Mind maps I'd expect to just see nav get bigger and the element to increase in size but not for the spatialization to change. Differentiate between spatial and non spatial apps."

The rule this round builds, to be written into docs/editing.md as a section of its own ("Pinch and size"):

1. **A pinch on a touch screen never zooms the browser.** The page's layout viewport stays the screen; nothing is ever wider than it unless it scrolls sideways on purpose (the bar, a table, a sheet).
2. **In a non-spatial app a pinch is the size of everything** (the drawer's stepper, kit/zoom.ts): the nav, the words, the cards and the dialogs grow or shrink together and the words reflow within the screen. Non-spatial: the home page, the Word Processor, the Outliner's list, the Presentation Builder, the Kanban Board, the Spreadsheet, the To-do List, the Calendar, the Calculator, the Website Builder's editor, the Trip Planner's list, every dialog and the drawer.
3. **In a spatial app a pinch zooms the picture, about the fingers, and two fingers pan it**; nothing in it reflows or moves relative to anything else; the nav keeps the size of everything. Spatial: the Drawing Tool's canvas, the Diagram Maker's picture, the Animator's stage, the Trip Planner's map (Leaflet's own pinch), and the Outliner's mind map and family tree when it is drawn as one.
4. **The size of everything reaches 250%** and its steps stay 10%; a pinch sets it continuously and the stepper rounds. **The nav has a natural limit** (Paul, 2026-10-10: "we should set a natural limit on how big nav can get when pinched"): the bar, the burger, the header, the drawer, the dialogs, the context bar, the menus and the pills grow with the size up to 150% and no further, while the words, the cards and the pictures go on to 250%. Past 150% a pinch enlarges the document alone; the nav stays the size a hand needs and no larger. At every size on a 390px screen the bar is one scrolling row, the burger opens the drawer, the drawer fits, dialogs fit and nothing scrolls sideways but what is meant to.
5. **Desktop is the browser's**: Ctrl/Mod with the wheel, a trackpad pinch, Mod and plus or minus are browser zoom, which reflows; the spatial canvases keep taking Mod+wheel for their own zoom as they do today. The viewport meta is not touched: no maximum-scale, no user-scalable (iOS ignores them and it would be wrong anyway); the system's accessibility zoom is untouched.

Tests live beside what they test; browser tests at 390×844 with real pointer events (PW_PORT=4444). `npx vitest run` whole once at the end.
