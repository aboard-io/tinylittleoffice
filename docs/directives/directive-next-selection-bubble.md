Paul: "In all apps: contextual menus should pop up when it makes sense."

Build, inside `<to-text>` so every host has it (Write, Deck's headline/words/notes, Outline rows, Board cards, Todo items): when the selection is a range of words (not collapsed, not inside a card, and the editor has the focus), a `<to-context>` appears centred under the selection (anchor = a function returning the selection's rect, `selRect(sel)` already exists) with: Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Highlight (pressed from `marksOf` over the selection), SEP, Link (pressed when the words are linked; opens the link bubble as `exec('link')` does), Footnote, SEP, and — only when the selection is whole blocks of kind p/li/h in a `doc` or `lines` editor — a list toggle (bulleted / numbered: `exec('ul')`/`exec('ol')`). It hides when the selection collapses or leaves the editor, while an IME composes, and when the link or note popover opens; it refreshes after each draw (the selection moves as marks are applied). Buttons never take the focus (the kit's do not). Hosts may turn it off with a `no-bubble` boolean attribute (Deck's headline may want to; leave defaults on). On a phone the native selection callout sits above the selection; ours sits below, inside the visual viewport (the kit places it) — check at 390px in the existing phone test that it does not cover the words selected.

Tests in test/browser/text.test.ts: select a range → the bubble is open under the selection with Bold unpressed; press Bold → the words are bold and the bubble shows Bold pressed and is still open; collapse the selection → closed; a range inside a card → closed; `no-bubble` → never opens; Link from the bubble opens the link popover and the bubble closes.
