# R4-J: Looks for the Word Processor, and templates throughout

Read first: directive-r4-common.md, paul-round-four-verbatim.md (messages 7 and 8), src/kit/app.ts (Template: id, name, blurb, doc), src/kit/home.ts (`<to-templates>`: the listbox with previews, Blank first), src/apps/deck/editor.ts (its Look menu: themes as a head field, the swatches), src/apps/write/{def,editor,view,model,templates}.ts, every other app's templates.ts, test/doc/corpus (every template is in the corpus and byte-stable), docs/rebuild.md (the house voice for templates: real, useful documents with real sample data, no lorem).

Paul: "The word processor needs visual templates that alter font and formatting for me. There should be all sans, all-serif, all monospace, mix of serif/sans. A dropdown like format on presentations (maybe same icon). We should also think of other document types like thesis, book review, news article, interview, music review, formal complaint, letter to politician, love letter, and so forth." And: "really increase the number of templates available throughout. It should feel quite considered and robust and exciting. Calculators too."

Part 1, the Word Processor's **Look** (one agent): a `look:` head field with named looks, each a type setting applied everywhere the document is drawn: the editor, `view()` pages, print/PDF, the HTML export (CSS), .docx (font names) and .rtf. The set: `sans` (the site's sans throughout), `serif` (a serif throughout: Georgia/Charter/Noto Serif stack), `mono` (a monospace throughout, typewriter feel, ragged right), `mixed` (serif headings, sans body), `inverse` (sans headings, serif body), plus two with more character: `book` (serif, justified, indented paragraphs, no gap between them, small caps headings) and `news` (narrow serif headline, sans body in two columns on paper). The bar gets a **Look** menu (the same icon Deck uses for its Look; swatch-less, each item a small two-line sample in its own type), the chosen one checked; the head field is byte-stable and absent means `sans`. Fonts are stacks of system fonts (no web fonts yet; the translation plan will add per-script stacks).
