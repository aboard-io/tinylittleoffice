# R8-sizing: lengths in rem and em, two scales, no zoom

Read first: directive-r8-common.md, src/kit/zoom.ts in full as it stands (BASE, size, navSize, applySize, pinchSize, keepSize, stepFrom, zoomed, zoomOf; --to-size and --to-nav-size), src/kit/pinch.ts (onPinch, pinchSizes: kept as they are), the six files that divide by zoomed()/zoomOf() (grep both), src/styles/kit.css in full (the nav list under one comment that R7-pinch made: `--to-nav-zoom` applied as `zoom`), src/styles/text.css, every src/apps/*/style.css, src/kit/drawer.ts's size row, test/browser/sizes.test.ts or wherever the round-seven fit tests live (grep `pinch` and `size(` in test/browser), test/browser/thumbs.test.ts (the preview boxes), scripts/make-cards.ts.

Why: the root's `zoom` scales fixed and absolute coordinates with it, so six modules divide what they measure by it, the nav needed a second zoom to stop at its limit, and each new placement is a new place to get it wrong. Lengths in rem scale with the root's font size and nothing else; the nav's own scale is a font size on its own elements and em inside them.

Build:
1. **Two scales.** zoom.ts keeps its public functions (size, navSize, pinchSize, keepSize, stepFrom, stepSize, SIZE_MIN/MAX, NAV_MAX, BASE) and loses zoomed and zoomOf. applySize sets two properties on the root and nothing else: `--to-doc` = BASE × size and `--to-nav` = BASE × navSize (both plain numbers), and the root's `font-size: calc(16px * var(--to-doc))` is the stylesheet's, not JS's. The six dividers divide by nothing: a measured rect is in CSS pixels and a `left`/`top` set from it is right as it is.
2. **The document in rem.** Every length in kit.css, text.css and the apps' stylesheets that is the document's or the page's (the paper, the words, cards, tables, rows, the home page's rows and previews, gaps and paddings around content) becomes rem at 16px to the rem (1px = 0.0625rem; a script does the conversion, you check the result; hairlines (1px borders) stay px). Font sizes in px become rem. Media queries stay in px (the viewport does not scale). The preview boxes (thumbs.test.ts) become rem and their test measures at the default size.
3. **The nav in em.** The nav list from round seven (`to-bar`, `.to-header`, the burger, the drawer, `.to-dialog` and the document dialogs, `<to-context>`, menus and the Download table, the link and note boxes, the completion list, the find bar, the pills, the Animator's sliders, the Drawing Tool's rails, and any app bar that joined) each sets `font-size: calc(16px * var(--to-nav))` on its root element, and every length inside is em (the same script, with /16 to em). Touch targets (`--to-touch`, 44px) are em in the nav and rem in the document. The one rule, in kit.css's header: *rem for the document, em inside a nav element, px only for hairlines and media queries.* A lint in test/ (a node test that greps the stylesheets) fails on a px length outside those uses.
4. **The pinch and the stepper** drive size() as today; the pill, the fit at every size, the nav's limit and the base of 1.3 all hold: the round-seven tests pass unchanged except where they read `zoom` or `--to-nav-zoom` (those read `--to-doc`/`--to-nav` or measured rects instead).
5. **Nothing outside the page changes**: pageSvg, the print frame, the PDF, the PNG, the thumbnails' drawings and the og cards are px-based SVG and untouched; a node test proves the pages of three documents are byte-identical before and after (commit the expected bytes' hashes).
6. docs/editing.md's "Pinch and size" says the mechanism in one sentence.

Tests (failing before where they can): the stylesheet lint; applySize sets `--to-doc` and `--to-nav` and no `zoom`; at size 2 on 390×844 a paragraph's computed font size is 2 × 1.3 × 16px and the bar's button height is 1.2 × 1.3 × 44px (the nav limit); a menu and the context bar open under their anchors at size 2 (the placement tests of round seven, now with no division); the fit tests; the page bytes.
