# R5-F: a thing in a mind map, org chart or family moves up or down the hierarchy, its children with it

Read first: directive-r4-common.md (the contract), docs/editing.md (§1 Tab/Shift+Tab: "in a list or an outline: indent/outdent the item (its family)"; the Outliner section; the Diagram Maker section), src/doc/ops.ts (family, moveFamily, placeFamily(doc, i, to, 'under' | 'after'), removeItem, depthOf), src/apps/diagram/model.ts (isTree, moveThing, moveAcross, moveItem, dropOn, childOf, Hit), src/apps/diagram/editor.ts (the keys in the words ~425 and on a focused thing, contextItems ~572, the Tab note at ~440 where Tab leaves the words), the Outliner's editor for how it does Indent/Bring out (its bar items, icons and keys are the model), the diagram tests.

Paul (2026-10-10): "In mind map I should be able to move an item up in the hierarchy—it becomes a child of its grandparent but brings its children."

Build, for the three tree kinds (mind, org, family):
1. Model: `promoteThing(doc, hit)`: the thing becomes a child of its grandparent, placed right after its parent, its whole family (children, grandchildren) carried with it, one level shallower each: the kernel's placeFamily with 'after' its parent does it; null for a root or a child of the root (nowhere to go: a root's children are already at the top). `demoteThing(doc, hit)`: the thing becomes the last child of the sibling before it, its family carried: placeFamily 'under' that sibling; null for a first child or a root. Both return { ops, hit } like moveThing so the editor can keep the thing in hand. Cross-links (`~ A -- B`) name things by their words, so they follow unchanged; check a family's "Ada + Charles | 1815" line carries its children.
2. Interface: Shift+Tab is Bring out and Tab is Indent, on a focused thing and inside its open words (the words commit first, as Enter does; today Tab leaves the words, which the standard reserves for places that are not a list or an outline; the focus stays on the thing, its words still open). The contextual bar for a tree thing gets Indent and Bring out (the Outliner's icons and labels, disabled when the move is impossible), placed as the Outliner places them. Alt+Up/Down (moveThing) stays as the move among siblings. Undo is one step. The other five kinds are untouched (Tab keeps leaving the words there).
3. docs/editing.md: in the Diagram Maker section, one sentence: in a tree, Tab/Shift+Tab indent and bring out a thing with its children, as in the Outliner.

Tests: model tests for promote/demote (a leaf, a thing with children and grandchildren, a root, a root's child, a first child, a family line, a cross-link that still resolves); a DOM test that Shift+Tab in a child's open words brings it under the grandparent and the words stay open on it; a bar test that the two items show and disable rightly.
