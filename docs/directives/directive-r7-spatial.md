# R7-spatial: the spatial canvases zoom about the fingers and pan with two

Read first: directive-r8-common.md and directive-r7-common.md, src/kit/pinch.ts as R7-pinch merged it (the `onPinch` reading of the fingers and the `[data-spatial]` opt-out), src/apps/draw/editor.ts (its own pinch ~197–210, 418–436, 498–500, 560; `scale()` ~713; zoomBy ~381; Mod+wheel ~1188), src/apps/diagram/editor.ts (zoom ~105–134, zoomBy and the wheel ~852–890, the picture's width rule ~881), src/apps/anim/editor.ts (zoom state, scale() ~497, onWheel ~599, the stage's width style ~688, `touch-action: none` in anim/style.css ~46), src/apps/outline/editor.ts and model.ts (the mind map and family tree drawings, ~584 and ~650 in model.ts: find how they are drawn and whether there is a canvas to pinch), src/apps/trip/editor.ts (Leaflet keeps its own pinch; nothing to build but the opt-out), src/apps/sheet/style.css's `touch-action: none` (~229: see what it is for; the sheet is non-spatial, a pinch there is the size).

Build:
1. **Each spatial canvas is marked `data-spatial`** so the kit's pinch leaves it alone: the Drawing Tool's canvas, the Diagram Maker's picture wrap, the Animator's stage wrap, the Trip Planner's map, the Outliner's mind map and family tree drawings. The bar, the fields and the dialogs around them are not marked: a pinch that begins on the bar is the size of everything.
2. **One reading of the fingers.** The Drawing Tool's own pinch is replaced by kit/pinch.ts's `onPinch` so every canvas reads the fingers the same way (the Drawing Tool keeps its quarter-step rounding and its 0.5–4 range). The Diagram Maker and the Animator gain the pinch: the picture's zoom follows the fingers' spread continuously (their existing ranges), **about the midpoint** (the point of the picture under the fingers stays under them: scroll the wrap by the difference the zoom made at that point), and two fingers moving together pan (scroll the wrap). Every canvas rounds to its step when the fingers lift, as the Drawing Tool does. A tap, a drag with one finger and the canvas's own tools behave as before: the pinch begins only with the second finger, and what the first finger began is let go, as the Drawing Tool already does.
3. **The Outliner drawn as a mind map or a family tree** is spatial: a pinch zooms the drawing in its wrap, about the fingers, and two fingers pan; the list it is drawn from stays non-spatial. If the drawing is an SVG scaled to its width today, give it the zoom state the Diagram Maker has (a width rule and Mod+wheel) so that there is one behaviour across the three pictures.
4. **The size of everything still grows the nav** around a spatial canvas, to the nav's limit of 150%: at size 2.5 on 390×844 the Drawing Tool's bar and tool rail, the Diagram Maker's bar and the Animator's sliders are at 150% and reachable (each joins kit.css's nav list rather than inventing its own rule), and the canvas is the same picture at the same zoom in a larger frame (nothing in it reflowed). Since round eight there is no root zoom and no zoomed(): a canvas's hit-testing divides pointer coordinates by its own `scale()` alone (CSS pixels to canvas units), and the Drawing Tool's `FINGER` reach is in CSS pixels; each canvas takes the scroll column's height (R8-shell), not the page's.
5. **docs/editing.md**'s "Pinch and size" section names the spatial canvases as built.

Tests (browser, 390×844, failing before): two synthetic touch pointers spreading over each canvas (Draw, Diagram, Anim, the Outliner's mind map) change that editor's zoom and not `size()`; the point under the midpoint stays within a few pixels; two fingers moving together scroll the wrap; a pinch on the bar beside the canvas changes `size()` and not the zoom; the Drawing Tool's existing pinch tests still pass through the shared reading; at size 2.5 the bars fit (the fit assertions of R7-pinch point 3, for these three apps).
