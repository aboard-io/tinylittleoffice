# R4-L: the Diagram Maker edits words in place; no popover for words anywhere

Read first: directive-r4-common.md; src/apps/draw/editor.ts (openWords/placeWords/onWordsInput/onWordsKey/closeWords: the overlay `<to-text shape=line>` positioned over the shape's words in the SVG, scaled with the picture, the SVG drawing that shape with blank words meanwhile, the context bar under the overlay, Enter commits, Shift+Enter breaks, Escape restores in the same undo step, blur commits; the R4-C branch made its labels rich); src/apps/diagram/{editor,model,view,style.css}.ts and tests (the `.dg-pop` manual popover with its `<to-text>`, `onPopKey`, the draft thing, the eight kinds: mind, org, family, lanes, timeline, venn, seating, net; lane names, edge labels, the join's words), docs/editing.md §5/§8.

Paul (2026-10-10): "I go into mind map and it pops up a freaking purple box instead of letting me type directly into the created box. This shouldn't happen anywhere."

1. **The Diagram Maker**: remove `.dg-pop`. A thing's words are edited in place: an overlay `<to-text shape=line>` laid exactly over the thing's words in the picture (position and font size from the SVG's text box and the picture's scale, as Draw's placeWords does; the thing's own text blank while editing; the overlay's box grows as words grow; the thing re-laid on commit), opened by the tap that makes or picks a thing (synchronously in the gesture so iOS raises the keyboard), with the placeholder selected on a new thing; Enter commits and makes the next thing as today's Enter did; Shift+Enter is a line break where the kind draws two lines ("Name | Role"); Escape restores the words from when editing began (one undo step) and keeps the thing in hand; a tap elsewhere commits; the thing's `<to-context>` sits under the overlay while editing. The same for lane names, timeline dates, a seating table's name, a join's words and the venn sets. Marks: the kinds' words are plain (keep `no-bubble`), unless the model already keeps runs. Every kind's DOM tests move from the popover to the overlay; the phone e2e checks the keyboard path (the overlay focused by the tap; WebKit cannot show the keyboard but can show focus).
2. **Everywhere**: walk every app for a popover or dialog whose purpose is a thing's words alone (grep `popover` and `to-dialog` in src/apps/*/editor.ts): the Calendar's event dialog has dates and fields (allowed, §8); the Website Builder's forms have URLs and icons (Paul asked for them); list every other case and convert it to in-place editing the same way, or report why not. Add to docs/editing.md §5 and §6 the rule: "Words are edited where they are drawn. A box over the picture for words alone is never used; a form is used only where a thing has fields that are not words (a date, an address, an icon)."
3. Strings, tests, tsc, i18n, the whole suite once. PW_PORT=4426.
