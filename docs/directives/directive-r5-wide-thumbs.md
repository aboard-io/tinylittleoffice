# R5-D: wide documents get wide thumbnails

Read first: directive-r4-common.md, src/kit/home.ts (the listbox thumb `.to-thumb`, 56×72 at src/styles/kit.css ~360; drive previews via <to-preview>), src/kit/preview.ts (56px tall, width from the page), src/kit/page.ts (Page, ViewOpts.small), src/kit/draw.ts (pageSvg), src/kit/search.ts / the hamburger's found list (.to-found to-preview 40px), the `view(doc, { small: true })` of the Spreadsheet (gridPage), Kanban Board, Diagram Maker, Drawing Tool, Animator, Calendar, Trip Planner, Presentation Builder (slides are landscape already: see how they fare), and the builders' notes: a ten-column sheet, a five-stage board and a seven-person family tree draw as thin strips in the phone previews.

Paul (2026-10-10) approved a landscape thumbnail for wide pages.

Build:
1. Kit: the listbox thumb and <to-preview> take the page's aspect: a portrait page draws as today (56×72 in the listbox); a landscape page is 72 tall and as wide as its aspect, clamped to twice its height (so a 16:9 slide is 128×72, a 4:3 page 96×72). The found list keeps its 40px height with the same rule. The listbox row's text column shrinks to fit (names ellipsise, blurbs wrap). No horizontal page scroll at 390px.
2. Apps: `view(doc, { small: true })` draws a wide document on a landscape page showing what can be read, not the whole thing shrunk: the Spreadsheet's small page is landscape and draws only the first columns that fit at a legible cell size (about six at 96 wide) and the rows that fit; the Kanban Board's small page shows up to three stages at full width; the Diagram Maker's and the Drawing Tool's small page takes the picture's bounds (landscape when the picture is wider than tall), the Animator's the stage's aspect; the Calendar's month and the Trip Planner's map are landscape if they are wider than tall. `small` must not change how a document prints, exports or shows on a slide (those pass no `small`; check that nothing else passes it). The Presentation Builder's slides already draw landscape: just confirm their thumbs now show wider.
3. Check on the dev server at 390px (WebKit) and desktop: the home listbox for the Spreadsheet (the mortgage template), the Kanban Board, the Diagram Maker (The Brontës), the Presentation Builder; and the drive previews under an app.

Tests: a kit test that the thumb width follows the aspect with the clamp; per-app view tests that `small` yields a landscape page for a wide document and leaves `view(doc)` unchanged (byte-equal SVG for the same document with and without the change for the non-small path); the shell test still green.
