# R4-M: words in the Website Builder's page, and footnotes at the foot

Read first: directive-r4-common.md; docs/editing.md §5/§6 (the rule: words are edited where they are drawn) and §8; the R4-L branch's work now on next (the Diagram Maker's `.dg-words` overlay, `wordsOver` in src/apps/draw/model.ts, docs/editing.md §8's exception for the Website Builder); src/apps/site/{editor,forms,view,model}.ts and tests, src/kit/page.ts (the generated page: one hashed script, the CSP; how the EDIT script is built and hashed), vite.config.ts (entries), public/_headers; src/kit/{text,note-pop}.ts, src/apps/write/{editor,view}.ts (the endnotes section), src/apps/deck/editor.ts (the slide's foot: `.deck-fn`), src/apps/outline/editor.ts (the notes under the outline), test/browser/text.test.ts (the footnote tests).

Paul (2026-10-10), on the two exceptions the R4-L report left: "Website builder—do it. That's the rule for footnotes." So: the page's words are typed in the page, inside the frame, and a footnote's words are typed where they are drawn, at the foot.

1. **The Website Builder**: headings, paragraphs, button words, card words and small-link words are edited in place inside the sandboxed frame. Build a frame-side editor bundle: a Vite entry (`src/apps/site/frame.ts`) that loads the kit's `<to-text>` (Lit and the kernel) into the frame page as one script whose hash goes into the frame's CSP as the EDIT script's does (page.ts already hashes scripts; the bundle is served from the site as a file and referenced by the frame's `<script src>`; for the downloaded site the editor is left out). Inside the frame a tap on a thing's words puts a `<to-text>` over them (shape=line for one-line things, a paragraph's `shape=lines` with Shift+Enter breaks; rich where the thing is rich, `no-bubble` where plain; the bubble works inside the frame since the kit's `<to-context>` is Lit too); each change is posted to the editor as the thing's new runs (the kernel's inline form) and becomes one `text` op on the block; Enter commits (and makes the next paragraph where that is the rule), Escape restores, a tap elsewhere commits; the keyboard rises on iPhone because the focus is set in the tap inside the frame. The mini forms stay only for things with fields that are not words (links' URLs, a picture's address, an icon); for a card or a button the words are typed in place and the URL comes from a small form opened by a URL chip on the thing's bar. Remove the §8 exception. The Insert at the top left, the bars, Preview and the display link stay. Tests: DOM tests with the frame (the site tests already drive the frame's script), the phone e2e (focus inside the frame on a tap, the words typed, the document changed).
2. **Footnotes at the foot** (kit + Write, Deck, Outline): the note's words are typed where they are drawn: the Word Processor's endnotes section at the end of the page, the slide's foot, the Outliner's notes under the outline. Each drawn note becomes a `<to-text shape=line>` (rich as a note is: marks and links, no blocks), editing the mark's `fn` string through the host (the host maps the note's index to the mark's position and emits the `text` op the note-pop emits today). The mark in the words: a tap or Enter on the chip scrolls the foot into view and focuses that note's words (the caret at the end); the Footnote tool inserts the mark and focuses the new empty note at the foot; Escape in a note returns to the mark; a note emptied and left removes its mark (as the pop did); Backspace at the start of an empty note removes it. `<to-note-pop>` is deleted with its strings and styles; test/browser/text.test.ts's footnote tests move to the hosts' tests (the kit editor keeps the chip, the `fn` run and the `text-note` event the host answers: define that event, `{ block, nth }` on a chip tap, replacing `openNoteOf`). Deck presenting still draws the notes at the slide's foot. docs/editing.md §2 says footnotes are typed at the foot.
