# R8-shell: a thin shell, the chrome of its own, and the app owns its scroll

Read first: directive-r8-common.md, src/kit/shell.ts in full (every render* method; onViewport/measure and `--to-vv-top`/`--to-stuck-h`/`scrolled`; the title row; the arrival strip; renderOther/renderDisplay; the dialogs: unlock, password, protect, share with post, embed, fit, which, ttl, maker, made, owned, qr, kept; the trust line; the keys dialog; onDocChange, writeLink, flushLink, keepSoon), src/styles/kit.css (the header, `to-bar`'s sticky rule, the `[scrolled]` rules and the `::before` strip, `.to-main`, `.to-header.titling`, the drawer, dialogs), src/kit/text.ts (keepCaretVisible, flushSel and every window.scroll*/scrollIntoView), src/kit/rows.ts (`scroller()` and `edges()`: the one place that already scrolls a container), src/kit/find.ts, src/kit/notes.ts, src/kit/complete.ts, src/kit/context.ts, src/kit/link-pop.ts, src/kit/menu.ts's `place`, src/apps/board/editor.ts, src/apps/trip/editor.ts, src/apps/site/frame.ts and view.ts (the Website Builder's iframe), src/apps/deck/editor.ts (presenting: full screen over everything), src/kit/drawer.ts, test/browser/shell.test.ts (the keyboard-offset test: replaced by this round's), test/browser/text.test.ts, test/browser/perf.test.ts, docs/kernel.md's shell section, docs/editing.md.

Paul, 2026-10-10: "when editing, the top nav vanishes except for the hamburger. The secondary nav is now aligned bottom." And the reason for the column: Safari on iOS does not shrink the page for the keyboard, only the visible window, so a bar stuck to the page's top has to be measured into place after events that come late or never.

Build:
1. **The split.** `<to-shell>` keeps the route, the document, the projection to the link (writeLink, flushLink, keepSoon), the library's keep, the title and the editor's mounting, and renders four elements: `<to-chrome>` (the header, the burger, the bar's place, the scroll column: everything about where things sit), `<to-share>` (the Share dialog with its post, embed, fit, display, ttl, short link, owned and qr parts), `<to-privacy>` (unlock, password, protect, signing, the trust line, "Privacy and keys"), and what is there already (the editor, `<to-drawer>`, `<to-find>`, `<to-toast>`, the keys dialog). Each new element gets its state through properties and speaks back through events (`to-share-open`, `to-lock`, …) named in its header; shell.ts ends under 500 lines and each of the others under 500. The home page, the arrival strip and "other" (a document from another app) go where they belong (chrome or shell), not into a fifth element.
2. **The page is the visible window, the column scrolls.** `html, body { height: 100%; overflow: hidden; overscroll-behavior: none }`; `<to-chrome>` is a column exactly the visual viewport's height (set from `visualViewport.height` on its resize, with `window.scrollTo(0, 0)` on the visual viewport's scroll so the layout viewport never slides) with the header at the top, the bar's slot, and `.to-main` as `overflow-y: auto; overscroll-behavior: contain` holding the document. A new `src/kit/scroll.ts` has `scrollerOf(el)` (moved from rows.ts) and `reveal(el, opts)`, and every scroll-to in the kit and the apps (text.ts's keepCaretVisible, find.ts, notes.ts, complete.ts, board, trip, the shell's own) goes through it; no module reads `window.scrollY` or scrolls the window (a node grep test fails on one). `--to-vv-top`, `--to-stuck-h`, `scrolled`, the `::before` strip, every `position: sticky` in kit.css that stuck something to the page and the shell's measure/onViewport code are deleted; the keyboard-offset test in shell.test.ts is replaced by this round's tests. Menus, the context bar, the link box and the completion list are placed within the column's box, not the page's.
3. **On a phone, editing.** Where the pointer is coarse (`(pointer: coarse)`): while a box has the focus (a `<to-text>`, a field in an app, the title), the header hides and the burger alone stays, floating at the column's top left on a card as it does today once scrolled; the bar sits at the column's bottom, so above the keyboard, as one scrolling row; the header returns when the focus leaves (a tap outside, Escape out of the box, Done). The drawer, since it is the one nav left, gets a "This document" section at its top with Share… and Download (the table), present only when a document is open. On a wide screen (fine pointer) the header stays and the bar is at the top under it, as today. Both in one `<to-chrome>` with one CSS rule per difference, no second element.
4. **What must still work in the column**: presenting a deck (full screen over the chrome), the Website Builder's iframe, the Drawing Tool's canvas and the Trip's map taking the column's height, the drawer and dialogs (top layer), the arrival strip (now at the column's top, under the header), print and the Display Link's pages (unchanged bytes), the Outliner's row drag scrolling the column, Skip to the document, Mod+F's find bar.
5. docs/kernel.md's shell section and docs/editing.md's phone paragraph say the new structure; the Safari cost (its toolbar no longer collapses on scroll) is written down there as the price paid.

Tests (browser, 390×844 with a coarse pointer emulated, and 1440×900; failing before): `window.scrollY` is 0 after scrolling a 300-paragraph document and `.to-main.scrollTop` is not; a faked visual viewport 300px shorter makes the column 300px shorter and the bar's bottom equal to the visual viewport's bottom, with no `--to-vv-top` anywhere; focus in the words hides the header and shows the burger and the bottom bar, a tap outside brings the header back; the drawer lists Share… and Download with a document open and not on the home page; typing at the end of a long document keeps the caret within the column (keepCaretVisible through scroll.ts); the Outliner's drag at the column's edge scrolls the column; presenting covers the chrome; the perf budget holds (perf.test.ts); the home page and every dialog at 390 and 1440 (the fit tests).
