# The Kanban Board's templates

Blank first (the app's own page: To do, Doing, Done), then the everyday ones, then work, then the school and
the street. Each is a real board with real cards and a title, canonical TLO (the corpus test holds every one
byte for byte, in test/doc/corpus/apps/board.json), using what a board has where it fits: a colour for each
stage, a limit on what a stage should hold (no template opens over one), a checklist on a card, a note in a
card's words, and the stages as rows.

- **A project**: a small studio's website, to do, doing with a limit of two, waiting on, and done, with checklists.
- **A week**: a week of errands and appointments, this week, today, waiting on someone else, done.
- **Moving house**: a move across Edinburgh by stage, the van booked and the kitchen packed as checklists, a note on the router.
- **Hiring**: candidates for a design job from applied to the offer, and the one it was not this time.
- **Content calendar**: a bakery's social posts for October, ideas to drafts to scheduled with dates to posted.
- **Editorial board**: a quarterly magazine's winter issue, each piece with its writer and length, pitch to page.
- **Sales pipeline**: a print shop's customers from lead to won or lost, each with the job and its price, the spec in a note.
- **Bug triage**: an app's point release, reported to fixed, with a limit on what is being fixed and reviewed at once.
- **Class projects**: a Year 8 class's science fair, from choosing the question to ready for the fair.
- **Mutual aid**: a street's needs and offers, matched and done, drawn as rows so each band reads across.
