# The Calendar's templates

Blank first (the app's own page: an empty calendar on this month), then the household, then the groups people run, then the year's seasons, then the work, then the events. Each is a real calendar a person could keep using, with its own title, a colour for each person or kind of thing (the head says what each stands for), repeats where things come round, and a footnote where a detail would crowd the day.

- **Family calendar** (`family`): a family's month: swimming and football repeating weekly, a birthday yearly, the dentist, a visit and the Christmas holidays, a colour each.
- **School term** (`school`): a primary school's autumn term from the INSET day to Christmas: closures, clubs, parents' evening, the trip, the fair and the nativity.
- **Bills and renewals** (`bills`): a household's direct debits on their days each month, the yearly renewals and the tax-return deadline, so nothing is a surprise.
- **Volunteer rota** (`rota`): who does which shift at a food bank, a colour for each volunteer, up to the Christmas hampers.
- **Holiday planner** (`holidays`): a small studio's time off, the bank holidays and the Christmas closure on one calendar, so leave does not clash.
- **League fixtures** (`fixtures`): a netball club's winter season: weekly training, home and away games in their colours, the cup round and the awards night.
- **Garden calendar** (`garden`): an allotment's year of sowing, planting, harvesting and pruning, every job repeating yearly.
- **Content schedule** (`content`): a bakery's posts, blog pieces and newsletters through the winter, with an advent story every day.
- **Editorial calendar** (`editorial`): two issues of a quarterly magazine from copy to sale: edit, layout, proofs, printer, launch, and the monthly meeting.
- **Training plan** (`training`, a schedule): twelve weeks to a half marathon in phases of weeks, the first ticked off, the race at the end.
- **Project plan** (`project`, a schedule): a café's website in phases with their tasks under them, how far along each is, the logo decision and the launch.
- **Conference agenda** (`conference`): two days of talks and workshops by the hour, a colour for each room, the dinner and the drinks the night before.
- **Festival weekend** (`festival`): a weekend of sessions at set times in Lisbon's own time zone, with a late set past midnight.
