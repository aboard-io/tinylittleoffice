# The Calendar's templates

Blank first (the app's own page: an empty calendar on this month), then the household, then the groups people run, then the year's seasons, then the work, then the events. Each is a real calendar a person could keep using, with its own title, a color for each person or kind of thing (the head says what each stands for), repeats where things come around, and a footnote where a detail would crowd the day. None names a time zone: a calendar with no zone is read in its reader's own, so a 5:30 pm shift is 5:30 pm wherever the calendar is opened.

- **Family calendar** (`family`): a New York family's month: swim lessons and soccer repeating weekly, a birthday yearly, the dentist, a visit, Thanksgiving and winter break, a color each.
- **School term** (`school`): a public elementary school's fall semester from the first day to winter break: days off, clubs, parent-teacher conferences, the field trip, the fair and the winter concert.
- **Bills and renewals** (`bills`): a household's autopays on their days each month, the yearly renewals and the tax deadlines, so nothing is a surprise.
- **Volunteer schedule** (`volunteers`): who does which shift at a Bronx food bank, a color for each volunteer, up to the holiday boxes.
- **Vacation planner** (`holidays`): a small studio's time off, the public holidays and the Christmas closing on one calendar, so time off does not clash.
- **League schedule** (`fixtures`): a youth soccer team's fall season: weekly practice, home and away games in their colors, the playoffs and the party.
- **Garden calendar** (`garden`): a community garden plot's year of sowing, planting, harvesting and pruning, every job repeating yearly.
- **Content schedule** (`content`): a bakery's posts, blog pieces and newsletters through the winter, with an advent story every day.
- **Editorial calendar** (`editorial`): two issues of a quarterly magazine from copy to sale: edit, layout, proofs, printer, launch, and the monthly meeting.
- **Training plan** (`training`, a schedule): twelve weeks to a half marathon in phases of weeks, the first checked off, the race at the end.
- **Project plan** (`project`, a schedule): a café's website in phases with their tasks under them, how far along each is, the logo decision and the launch.
- **Conference agenda** (`conference`): two days of talks and workshops by the hour, a color for each room, the dinner and the drinks the night before.
- **Festival weekend** (`festival`): a weekend of sessions at set times in Brooklyn, with a late set past midnight.
