# The Word Processor's templates

Blank first (the app's own page), then the everyday ones, then the writing with a shape of its own. Each is a
real document with real sample data, canonical TLO (the corpus test holds every one byte for byte), and where a
look fits it, it names one in its head (`look:`, look.ts).

- **A letter**: a business letter from a studio to a client, setting down what was agreed.
- **Cover letter** (serif and sans): applying for a job, in four short paragraphs.
- **Memo**: one change at a studio, why (with its figure in a footnote), and what is needed by when.
- **Meeting notes**: agenda, notes, decisions and actions as tasks to tick.
- **Minutes**: a tenants' association meeting: who came, the treasurer's sums in a table, a motion and its vote.
- **Résumé**: name, experience, education and skills on a page.
- **One-page CV** (typewriter): a backend engineer's CV, set in monospace.
- **Invoice**: head fields for from and to, lines in a table whose amounts, subtotal, tax and total are formulas.
- **Contract**: a plain-words agreement: what each party does, and how it ends.
- **Newsletter** (serif and sans): a block association's month, with icons and a table of dates.
- **Recipe** (sans and serif): ingredients in a table that scales from four to six by formula, steps, a footnote.
- **Questions and answers**: a community garden's questions, each a heading over its answer.
- **Press release**: a library reopening: the lead, the detail, a quote, opening hours, a press contact.
- **News article** (newspaper): headline, byline, two columns on paper, and a chart of the figures as a held spreadsheet.
- **Interview** (serif and sans): an introduction, then named questions and answers.
- **Book review** (serif): *Middlemarch* for a book club: stars, what it is about, what worked, a quote.
- **Music review** (sans and serif): an album: label and length, stars, the songs, the best tracks.
- **Thesis** (book): title page, abstract, a page break, chapters with footnotes, a table of changes worked out, a bibliography.
- **Grant proposal** (serif and sans): a repair café: the ask, the need, the plan, a budget that sums, measures.
- **Syllabus** (serif and sans): a history course: times, aims, a week-by-week table, grade weights summing to 100%.
- **Formal complaint**: a cancelled train: what happened, in order; what is wanted; a deadline; a footnote.
- **Letter to a politician** (serif): a parent asks their representative to vote for a bill, and asks how they will.
- **Love letter** (serif): ordinary things missed, across the distance.
- **Wedding speech**: the best woman's speech: how they met, what she has seen, a word to the groom, the toast.
- **Eulogy** (serif): a grandmother's life in a few minutes.
- **Zine essay** (typewriter): a personal essay about the night bus, with a highlighted line.
- **Petition**: what is asked, why (with a footnote to the evidence), and a table of names.
- **Terms of service**: a dog walker's terms: booking, a price table, keys, insurance in a footnote, ending.
