# The To-Do List's templates

Blank first (the app's own page, one empty task), then the everyday lists, then the ones for a project or
an occasion. Each is a real list with its own title and real sample data, canonical TLO (the corpus test
holds every one byte for byte), and each uses what the app can do: checks, stars on what matters, due dates
in the order they fall, rich words (bold aisles and seasons, italic book titles) and, in the wedding, a note.

- **This week** (`week`): a working week's errands by the day, the important ones starred, one already done.
- **Daily routine** (`daily`): the habits of a day, to check off each morning and clear at night.
- **Shopping** (`shopping`): a quick trip to the store, the one thing for Saturday starred and dated.
- **Groceries by aisle** (`groceries`): the weekly grocery run in the order of the aisles, each line led by its aisle.
- **Reading list** (`reading`): a season's books, favorites starred, the book-club and film-night ones dated.
- **Packing for a trip** (`trip`): what to take to Lisbon and what to do before the taxi at 5:15 am.
- **Garden by season** (`garden`): a year's jobs from fall 2026 on, each dated to its season.
- **Project launch** (`launch`): an app's last weeks from feature freeze to go-live, in the order they fall due.
- **Onboarding** (`onboarding`): a new hire's first two weeks, from the door code to the first one-on-one.
- **Moving out** (`moveout`): notice to keys back: the truck, the mail, the cleaning, the deposit.
- **Wedding checklist** (`wedding`): the last months before a December wedding, with a note on the final numbers.
