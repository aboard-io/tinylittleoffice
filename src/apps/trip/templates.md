# The Trip Planner's templates

Blank first (an empty map to tap), then the everyday trips (a weekend in a city, a road trip, an afternoon of food, a day's walk, a ride, a tour of campuses), then the four that were here, then the long and special journeys. Every one is a real route with real coordinates and 2026 dates where a day matters, a note at every stop; between them they use every way the app measures: kilometers, miles, hours of driving, and places as dots with no route.

- **A weekend in Copenhagen** (`weekend`): Friday night to Sunday's train on foot, in kilometers; the shape of any city break.
- **Iceland's Ring Road** (`iceland`): ten days around the island by car, each leg in hours of driving; a loop that ends where it began.
- **A Dublin food crawl** (`crawl`): seven places to eat in one afternoon, west to east, the legs short enough to walk.
- **Yosemite's Mist Trail loop** (`hike`): a day hike past two waterfalls and back, in miles, with water and timing notes.
- **The Loire by bike** (`cycling`): Orléans to Saumur in six days, château to château, in kilometers.
- **A college tour** (`colleges`): six New England campuses in a week, the tour booked at each, in hours of driving.
- **A week in Portugal** (`portugal`): Lisbon to Porto by car, in kilometers, a tip at every stop.
- **Down the Pacific coast** (`coast`): San Francisco to Los Angeles on Highway 1, in miles.
- **The Scottish Highlands** (`highlands`): a loop from Edinburgh, each leg in hours of driving.
- **Rome, the places to see** (`rome`): places as dots, not a route: what to see and when to go.
- **Japan by rail** (`japan`): Tokyo to Miyajima in the cherry blossom, in kilometers.
- **The last 100 km of the Camino** (`camino`): the pilgrimage from Sarria to Santiago, a stage a day, each stage's distance in its note.
- **New York in the movies** (`films`): seven film and TV locations, uptown to downtown, at their real addresses.
- **A wedding on Lake Como** (`wedding`): dots for the guests: the ceremony, the welcome drinks, where to sleep, the station and the airport.
