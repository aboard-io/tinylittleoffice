# Spreadsheet templates

Blank comes first (the app's own page); then these, in the order the list shows them. Each is a real
document with its own title, written by the kernel so it round-trips byte for byte (the corpus test
holds every one).

- **Budget**: one month planned against actual, a line each, the difference and the totals.
- **Household budget by month**: six months of a home's bills and spending, totalled across and down, against the pay.
- **Expense log**: what was spent, when and on what kind of thing (an option column), totalled.
- **Savings goal**: what went in each month and the running total; the goal a named cell, what is still to go drawn large.
- **Habit tracker**: four habits ticked day by day for two weeks; the share of each day kept and the days each habit was kept.
- **Reading list**: books read, being read and next (an option column), the titles in italics, the days each took and the pages read.
- **Workout log**: sets, reps and weight for each lift, the volume worked out, how each felt.
- **Training log**: anything practised, a line a time, with a total for each kind of thing (SUMIF).
- **Time sheet**: a week's hours by client and task; the billable ones (a tick) priced at their rate.
- **Trip costs split**: who paid for what on a weekend for four, each one's share, and who owes whom.
- **Project tracker**: tasks with owners, dates counted from today, a status and a tick when done.
- **Schedule with dates**: a job in steps, each starting when the last ends, with the end dates and working days.
- **Grade book**: a class's marks, each student's average and letter grade (nested IF), and the class average.
- **Membership roll**: a community garden's members, when they paid and renew (EDATE), who holds a key, the dues in.
- **Seating count**: a wedding's tables, seats and guests, the meals chosen and a check that they add up.
- **Poll results**: the answers and their votes, a pie above them.
- **Fundraising thermometer**: what has come in week by week against the goal, a bar chart above, the total drawn large.
- **Invoice ledger**: every invoice sent, when it was due and paid, what is still owed and how many days late.
- **Inventory**: stock on hand against its reorder point, what it is worth, and a flag when it is low.
- **Petty cash**: money in and out of the tin, the balance after each, a tick for each receipt.
- **Rate card**: a freelancer's services priced from one hourly rate, with a rush price; change the rate, every price follows.
- **Mortgages compared**: four offers on one house, the monthly payment (PMT), all in with tax, and the interest paid.
- **Loans compared**: amount, rate and years for each loan, the monthly payment, the total and the interest.
- **Unit converter**: miles, pounds, oven temperatures, cups, gallons and more; type an amount, read the other unit.
- **Tournament bracket**: eight teams in a knockout, the winners worked out and carried on to the final.
- **Month by month, with a chart**: a shop's sales this year against last, drawn as lines.
- **A subscription business**: visitors to signups to paying to retained, as flows (Sankey), the assumptions to change.
- **A café**: customers and spend make the month's takings; supplies, rent, wages and profit flow out.
- **A freelancer**: hours, rate and weeks make the year's invoices; expenses and tax come off, take-home is left.
- **A mutual aid fund**: donors and gifts fill the fund; grants, admin and a reserve come out.
