# Presentation Builder templates

Blank comes first (the app's own page); then these, in the order the list shows them: the decks people
make most, then the occasions, then the printed things, two of each format. Each is a real document with
its own title and a look of its own, written by the kernel so it round-trips byte for byte (the corpus test
holds every one).

- **A talk**: a twenty-minute meetup talk: title, agenda, three points with speaker notes, a footnote, a close (Midnight).
- **Status update**: a week's project status: done and next side by side, risks, asks (Boardroom).
- **A proposal**: a pitch to one client: what you heard, what you propose, when, what it costs (Ivory).
- **A pitch**: a seed round in eight slides: the problem with its source, the product, traction on a line chart, the business, the team, the ask (Graphite, slide transition).
- **A lesson**: a seventh-grade science lesson: aims, what they know, the model, the phases in two columns, a big-icon slide, a check, homework, the check's answers last; the teacher's notes under each (Chalkboard).
- **Conference talk**: a talk with speaker notes on every slide, a bar chart of results, a footnote, and the lessons to write down (Ocean).
- **Quarterly review**: a bike shop's quarter for its partners: revenue by month on a chart, wins and misses, a table of the numbers that matter, next quarter, decisions (green icons).
- **Workshop agenda**: a Saturday repair workshop: the schedule as a table, a to-do list of what to bring on the slide, the steps, two columns of fixes (Confetti).
- **Product launch**: a kettle in a sentence, what it does, prices in a money table, the launch dates, the press contact (orange icons, zoom).
- **Town-hall notice**: a residents' meeting about a playground: why, what is proposed, the budget as a pie, when, how to have a say and access (Sunset).
- **Trivia night**: a trivia night in four rounds, a score table that adds itself up, the answers on the last slide, and no closing QR code (so the room cannot read ahead) (Arcade).
- **Wedding slideshow**: a couple's story for the reception, from the bookstore to the question, a timeline, thank-you; no QR code (Bubblegum, red icons).
- **Zine**: Small Joys, eight short pages about a morning.
- **Recipe zine**: seven soups for a cold week, one a page.
- **Booklet**: a studio's handbook of how it works.
- **Concert program**: a community choir's winter concert: welcome, both halves, notes on the music with a footnote, the singers, thanks, and the words to sing.
- **Brochure**: a web studio's trifold: what we make, how it goes, prices, who we are, contact.
- **Farm brochure**: a pick-your-own farm's trifold: what grows when, how it works, prices, the barn, how to find it.
- **Comic**: Biscuit the dog's morning walk, in panels.
- **Comic: the umbrella**: a borrowed umbrella that becomes a friendship, in panels.
- **Poster**: an open mic night flyer.
- **Lost cat poster**: a lost-pet poster in red: name, description, where last seen, a number.
- **Name badges**: six badges for a meetup.
- **Conference badges**: six badges with roles (speaker, organizer, volunteer), on the Ocean look.
