# The Outliner's templates

Blank first (the app's own page), then the everyday ones, then study and work, then the shapes another app
draws from the menu, and last the trees you walk by answering yes or no. Each is a real outline with real
sample data and a title, canonical TLO (the corpus test holds every one byte for byte, in
test/doc/corpus/apps/outline.json), using what the Outliner has where it fits: footnotes, numbered items,
named lines to the parent, joins between branches, and a sheet under an item.

- **Packing list**: five days in Lisbon by category, with notes on shoes and plugs, and a numbered list for before leaving.
- **A decision**: should we move the office; each option with what speaks for and against it, the rent in a footnote.
- **Launch plan**: a product launch, its product, marketing and support, the dates, and joins where one branch feeds another.
- **A talk**: twenty minutes in three timed parts; "Show as slides" makes a slide of each top item.
- **A speech**: a five-minute retirement toast, its parts timed on their lines and its three stories numbered.
- **An essay plan**: a thesis, two arguments with their evidence (a citation in a footnote), the objection and the answer, joined.
- **A research plan**: a question, a numbered method, a budget as a sheet under its item that adds itself up, a timeline, risks and reading.
- **A syllabus**: a ten-week course, a numbered topic a week, the marking and the reading.
- **A novel**: the people and three acts of chapters, joined where a person turns a chapter.
- **A book**: parts and chapters of a book of ideas, a line under each for what it argues.
- **A company**: a bakery of nine as "Name (role)"; "Draw as an org chart" gives each box its name over its role.
- **A family**: three generations and the next as "Name (years)"; "Draw as a family tree" gives each its name over its years.
- **A decision tree**: indoors or out for a Saturday market, each answer a yes or a no on its line.
- **Troubleshooting**: no internet at home, a yes-or-no question at each step down to the call to make.
