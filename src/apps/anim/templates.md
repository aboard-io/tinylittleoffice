# The Animator's templates

Blank first (the app's own empty stage), then the everyday motions (a ball, a spinner, a title, a card, the memes), then the ones that tell something (a word to learn, the weather, a countdown, a route, a sign), then the scene. Each starts on a frame worth seeing, because the home page's preview is the first frame; each is a drawing a person could keep working on, in Draw mode or in Move.

- **Lift off** (`liftoff`): the smallest motion there is, two shapes and two keys each; the one to learn keys from.
- **Bouncing ball** (`bounce`): the fall alone, its keys spaced like gravity, played forward and back so it bounces forever.
- **Loading spinner** (`spinner`): eight dots swelling in turn, for a page or a slide that says something is happening.
- **Title card** (`curtain`): curtains part on a film festival's name, played once, resting on the title: an opening for a video or a talk.
- **Birthday card** (`birthday`): a hopping present, swaying stars and hearts, and the greeting in rainbow letters, to send as a GIF.
- **Quick standup** (`standup`): a meme with a caption above and below in impact type and a clock spinning between.
- **Build is green** (`green`): a meme for a team chat, the trophy spinning in, landing and glowing.
- **Flash card** (`flip`): a Spanish word that turns over to its English and back, made with the turn about the upright axis.
- **Weather this week** (`weather`): four days of Lisbon's forecast, each icon moving in its own way.
- **New Year countdown** (`countdown`): three, two, one, then the greeting, played once, for a party's screen or invitation.
- **Flight path** (`flight`): a plane along a dotted route between two pins, for telling people when you land.
- **Neon sign** (`neon`): a café's name flickering by depth, its hours, and a glowing cup, for a store window or a menu board.
- **Sunrise** (`sunrise`): a scene with a pen-drawn skyline, the sun rising behind it and the words sliding in.
