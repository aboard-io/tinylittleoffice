# The Drawing Tool's templates

Blank first (the app's own empty page), then the everyday drawings, then the diagrams of systems, then
pixel art. Each is a real drawing with real sample data, canonical TLO (the corpus test holds every one
byte for byte), and each uses what the app can do where it fits: colours and fills, text sizes, rich
words, icons, joins that follow their shapes, turned shapes, shapes drawn by their corners, and a
background tile.

- **Flowchart** (`flowchart`): a shop's refund rule as questions and answers, every way ending in the returns book.
- **Birthday card** (`card`): a ninetieth birthday card, tilted, on a confetti background tile.
- **Event map** (`event`): a street party from above: stage, stalls, first aid, toilets and a meeting point.
- **Floor plan** (`floorplan`): a one-bedroom flat, rooms labelled with their sizes, doors as gaps.
- **Room plan** (`room`): one room's furniture to scale, a turned bookcase and television, the door's swing drawn by its corners.
- **Wireframe** (`wireframe`): a library app's phone screen in boxes, with notes joined to the parts they explain.
- **Logo sketch** (`logo`): three marks for a coffee roaster side by side, the name set large, a date to choose by.
- **Badge** (`badge`): a volunteer-of-the-year rosette with turned ribbons, to print and pin on.
- **Order to doorstep** (`order`): a parcel's journey in steps with icons, and the table the payment is written to.
- **Request flow** (`request`): a browser, a server and the data: the smallest system diagram.
- **Three tiers** (`tiers`): phones and laptops, an API, a database and a cache.
- **Pixel heart** (`heart`): a 16 by 16 heart to colour in.
- **Weather icons** (`icons`): sun, cloud, rain and storm, four 16 by 16 icons on one 32 grid.
- **Game sprite** (`sprite`): Pip the frog, a 32 by 32 outlined character.
- **Background tile** (`tile`): an 8 by 8 repeating pattern, to use behind a drawing or any page.
