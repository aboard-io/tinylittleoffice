# The Website Builder's templates

Blank first (the kit's: an empty site), then the everyday sites, then the Quick Links pages. Each is a real
site with real words, a theme that suits it, and what a site has where it fits: pages and sections, buttons,
big links (one to a ticket each on the band's tour), pictures, a map, a shop with a cart where something is
for sale, a table with its sums, another document held in a page (a list, a QR code), a footnote. Each is
canonical TLO, held byte for byte by the corpus test (test/doc/corpus/apps/site.json and every app's templates).

- **A café**: Harbor Coffee on the quay, what is special, the menu, how to find it.
- **A small shop**: Quay Pottery's mugs and bowls with a cart that emails the order, and the Saturday classes.
- **An event**: a two-day book fair in a tent on the harbour, the programme and the way there.
- **A portfolio**: Iona Reid's Highland photographs page by page, signed prints for sale, commissions.
- **A wedding**: Priya and Sam on 5 December 2026, the day hour by hour, a map, where to stay, how to reply.
- **A band**: the Harbour Lights' new record, four tour dates each linked to its tickets, merch with a cart.
- **A clinic**: a family practice in Leith, hours, out of hours and emergencies, services, the way in.
- **A campaign**: keeping a branch library open, three numbers, the cost added up, the hearing, a QR code for the petition.
- **A mutual-aid group**: Elm Street Neighbours, how to ask, how to offer, what the group still needs.
- **A school club**: a robotics club, when it meets, the regional final, T-shirts and brownies for the coach, what to bring.
- **A newsletter sign-up**: an allotment letter, one button to subscribe, the last three letters linked.
- **A holiday cottage**: a cottage in Port Isaac, the rooms, prices by season worked out per night, booking, a map.
- **A plumber**: a family firm in Truro, what they do, what it costs, when to ring at night.
- **A yoga studio**: the week's classes, passes to buy with a cart, the teachers.
- **Quick Links: a maker**: a potter's picture, shop, classes and inbox; the places she posts at the foot.
- **Quick Links: a group**: a mutual-aid group's ways to ask, help, meet and chat.
- **Quick Links: a band**: the new record, the dates, the merch, every place the music is.
- **Quick Links: a small business**: a café's menu, a table booked, the way there, the phone.
- **Quick Links: a writer**: the newsletter, the book, the latest piece, a way to say hello.
- **Quick Links: one link**: one thing to say and one button; here, a leaving do.
