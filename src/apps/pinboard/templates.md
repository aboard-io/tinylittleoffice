# The Pinboard's templates

Blank first (the app's own: the board's empty table), then these. Each is a real board with real sample content,
canonical TLO (the corpus test holds every one byte for byte), set in New York where set anywhere, and each uses what
the app can do where it fits: frames in colors holding notes, words on nothing, a pen's stroke, arrows with words and
a line. None holds a picture, since the site keeps none; a host's own board pins its frame grabs.

- **Film board** (`film`): a seminar's board on Rear Window, its two acts in frames, what happens on notes, the motifs, the questions for Thursday, and arrows from each clue to the next.
- **Mood board** (`mood`): a student short's palette, light, sound and places, a frame each, with arrows where one feeds another.
- **Retro** (`retro`): a crew's retro after a subway shoot: what went well, what did not, and what to try next, each problem joined to its fix.
