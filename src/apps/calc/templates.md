# The Calculator's templates

Blank first (the app's own empty tape), then the everyday sums, then money over time and the body, then the graphs. Each is a real tape a person could keep working on; a sentence ending in a period or a colon is a note on the tape, with no answer beside it.

- **Splitting the bill** (`bill`): a dinner for six, the tip added and the total shared.
- **A shopping total** (`shopping`): a market run with things bought by weight, the total and the change.
- **Tax on a price** (`tax`): New York City sales tax added to a price, and taken back out of a receipt.
- **Percentage change** (`percent`): a rent rise in dollars and percent, over a year, and against a pay raise.
- **Kitchen conversions** (`kitchen`): cups, oven temperatures, spoons and weights converted.
- **Scaling a recipe** (`recipe`): a recipe for eight made for twelve, each amount kept by name.
- **Unit conversions** (`units`): length, weight and temperature, metric and imperial both ways.
- **Currency at a rate you type** (`currency`): a trip costed in euros, turned into dollars at a typed rate, with the card fee.
- **Fuel for a trip** (`fuel`): miles, miles per gallon and the price of gas, split four ways.
- **Paint for a room** (`paint`): wall area less the openings, two coats, rounded up to whole gallons.
- **Time zones** (`zones`): one meeting hour in six cities, on a 24-hour clock.
- **Running pace** (`pace`): a 10K time as minutes a kilometer and a mile, and the longer races at that pace.
- **Body mass index** (`bmi`): metric and imperial, with a note that it is a guide.
- **Dose by weight** (`dose`): milligrams a kilogram turned into milliliters of a syrup, with a plain warning that it is arithmetic, not advice.
- **Electricity for an appliance** (`power`): watts and hours to kilowatt-hours, and what they cost.
- **Loan payment** (`loan`): the monthly payment of a car loan and the interest paid over its life.
- **Compound interest** (`savings`): a lump sum and monthly savings grown, against what went in.
- **Break-even** (`break-even`, a graph): costs against sales; where the lines cross.
- **A curve and its slope** (`slope`, a graph): a cubic, its derivative, and the tangent where the slope is zero.
- **Waves adding up** (`waves`, a graph): sine harmonics summed toward a square wave.
