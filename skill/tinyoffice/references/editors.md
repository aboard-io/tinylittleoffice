# Driving the editors

For an agent working in the editors in a browser: the keys each app answers, written from the apps' command registry (`Mod` is ⌘ on a Mac and Ctrl elsewhere; `at:` places are inside the app). Every command in force is also found by the menu's search (the hamburger), which Mod+K opens focused outside words.

- **Everywhere (the page)**: `Mod+Z` Undo; `Mod+Shift+Z`/`Mod+Y` Redo; `Mod+F` Find in this document; `Mod+S` Save to this device; `Mod+Shift+S` Share; `Mod+Shift+D` Download; `Mod+P` Print, or save as PDF; `Mod+/` Show these shortcuts; `Mod+K` Find app, doc, template, or command.
- **Words (wherever words are typed)**: `Mod+X` Cut; `Mod+C` Copy; `Mod+V` Paste; `Mod+B` Bold; `Mod+I` Italic; `Mod+U` Underline; `Mod+Shift+X` Strikethrough; `Mod+Shift+H` Highlight; `Mod+E` Code; `Mod+K` Link; `Tab` Indent; `Shift+Tab` Outdent; `Alt+ArrowUp` Move up; `Alt+ArrowDown` Move down; `Mod+Enter` Check or uncheck a task; `Mod+A` Select all (this field first, then the whole document).
- **Rows chosen whole**: `Enter` Edit; `Escape` Close or deselect; `Alt+ArrowUp` Move up; `Alt+ArrowDown` Move down; `Tab` Indent; `Shift+Tab` Outdent; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Mod+C` Copy; `Mod+X` Cut; `Mod+V` Paste; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `Shift+ArrowUp`/`Shift+ArrowDown` Extend selection; `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` Back to text; `Mod+A` Select all (this field first, then the whole document).
- **Word Processor**: `Mod+Shift+Enter` Page break; `Mod+Shift+L` Align left; `Mod+Shift+E` Center; `Mod+Shift+R` Align right; `Mod+Shift+J` Justify; `Enter` Edit; `Alt+ArrowUp` Move up; `Alt+ArrowDown` Move down; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Mod+C` Copy; `Mod+X` Cut; `Mod+V` Paste; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `Escape`/`ArrowDown`/`ArrowRight` Back to text; `ArrowUp`/`ArrowLeft` Back to text.
- **Outliner**: `Tab` Indent; `Shift+Tab` Outdent; `Alt+ArrowUp` Move up; `Alt+ArrowDown` Move down; `Mod+Shift+,` Zoom out; `Mod+Shift+.` Zoom in; `Mod+Enter` Check or uncheck a task; `Mod+Alt+ArrowUp`/`Mod+Alt+ArrowDown` Collapse or expand; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` Next or previous.
- **Presentation Builder**: `Mod+Alt+Enter` Present; `Alt+ArrowUp` Move slide up; `Alt+ArrowDown` Move slide down; `Mod+Shift+Enter` A new slide; `Mod+Enter` Done; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `PageUp`/`PageDown` Previous or next slide; `ArrowRight`/`Space`/`PageDown`/`Enter` Next line or slide (presenting); `ArrowLeft`/`PageUp`/`Backspace` Back (presenting); `End` Whole slide (presenting); `N`/`Shift+N` Notes; `Escape` Stop presenting.
- **Spreadsheet**: `Mod+Alt+=` Row above; `Mod+D` Fill down from the top row; `Mod+C` Copy; `Mod+X` Cut; `Mod+V` Paste; `Delete`/`Backspace` Clear cells; `Alt+ArrowUp` Move up; `Alt+ArrowDown` Move down; `Mod+Alt+-` Delete ; `Mod+A` Select all (this field first, then the whole document); `Mod+Enter` Done; `Tab`/`Shift+Tab` Indent or outdent a list item; next cell in a table; `F2` Edit cell with cursor at end; `Shift+ArrowUp`/`Shift+ArrowDown`/`Shift+ArrowLeft`/`Shift+ArrowRight` Extend selection; `Space` Check or uncheck a task; `Escape` Close or deselect; `Escape` Restore cell value; `Enter`/`Shift+Enter`/`ArrowDown`/`ArrowUp`/`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` Next or previous.
- **Calculator**: `Enter` Done; `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown` Next or previous; `Escape` Clear the line; `Mod+A` Select all (this field first, then the whole document); `Enter` Done; `Escape` Close or deselect; `Alt+ArrowUp`/`Alt+ArrowDown` Move the selected items up or down; `Shift+ArrowUp`/`Shift+ArrowDown` Extend selection; `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown` Next or previous; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Mod+A` Select all (this field first, then the whole document); `Backspace` Delete.
- **Drawing Tool**: `V` Select; `R` Rectangle; `E` Ellipse; `D` Diamond; `L` Line; `A` Arrow; `T` Text; `K`/`Shift+K` Join two shapes; `P` Pen; `F` Freehand; `[` Rotate left; `]` Rotate right; `Enter` Edit text; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Mod+C` Copy; `Mod+X` Cut; `Mod+V` Paste; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `Enter` Done; `Escape` Close or deselect; `Backspace` Delete; `Escape` Close or deselect; `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`Enter` Join two shapes; `Mod+A` Select all (this field first, then the whole document); `Mod+Shift+L` Align: left; `Mod+Shift+E` Align: center; `Mod+Shift+R` Align: right; `Alt+ArrowUp`/`Alt+ArrowDown` Move the selected items up or down; `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`Shift+ArrowLeft`/`Shift+ArrowRight`/`Shift+ArrowUp`/`Shift+ArrowDown`/`Mod+ArrowLeft`/`Mod+ArrowRight`/`Mod+ArrowUp`/`Mod+ArrowDown` Nudge selected shapes; `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown` Tap or drag to paint; the arrow keys move, Enter paints.; `Enter`/`Space` Tap or drag to paint; the arrow keys move, Enter paints..
- **Pinboard**: `V` Select; `N` Note; `T` Text; `F` Frame; `P` Pen; `A` Arrow; `Enter` Edit text; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Mod+X` Cut; `Mod+C` Copy; `Mod+V` Paste; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `Escape` Close or deselect; `Mod+A` Select all (this field first, then the whole document); `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`Shift+ArrowLeft`/`Shift+ArrowRight`/`Shift+ArrowUp`/`Shift+ArrowDown` Nudge selected shapes.
- **Diagram Maker**: `Alt+ArrowUp` Move up; `Alt+ArrowDown` Move down; `Tab` Indent; `Shift+Tab` Outdent; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Mod+C` Copy; `Mod+X` Cut; `Mod+V` Paste; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `Enter`/`Space` Edit; `Mod+Alt+ArrowLeft` Move left; `Mod+Alt+ArrowRight` Move right; `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` Next or previous; `ArrowDown`/`ArrowRight` Next or previous; `Escape` Close or deselect; `Mod+Enter` Done.
- **Animator**: `Space` Play; `K` Add keyframe; `Enter` Edit text; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Mod+C` Copy; `Mod+X` Cut; `Mod+V` Paste; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `,`/`.` Previous or next frame; `Home`/`End` First or last frame; `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`Shift+ArrowLeft`/`Shift+ArrowRight`/`Shift+ArrowUp`/`Shift+ArrowDown`/`Mod+ArrowLeft`/`Mod+ArrowRight`/`Mod+ArrowUp`/`Mod+ArrowDown` Nudge selected shapes, or step one frame; `Mod+A` Select all (this field first, then the whole document); `Escape` Close or deselect.
- **Calendar**: `Mod+Alt+ArrowLeft` A day earlier; `Mod+Alt+ArrowRight` A day later; `Alt+Enter` Edit dates; `Tab`/`Shift+Tab` Indent or outdent a list item; next cell in a table; `Alt+ArrowUp`/`Alt+ArrowDown` Move the selected items up or down; `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown` Next or previous; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `Mod+Enter` Check or uncheck a task; `Mod+Alt+ArrowLeft` A day earlier; `Mod+Alt+ArrowRight` A day later; `Alt+Enter` Edit dates; `Enter` Event; `Escape` Close or deselect; `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`Shift+ArrowLeft`/`Shift+ArrowRight`/`Shift+ArrowUp`/`Shift+ArrowDown`/`Home`/`End`/`PageUp`/`PageDown` Next or previous; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `Mod+D` Duplicate.
- **Trip Planner**: `Mod+Alt+Enter` Present; `Alt+ArrowUp`/`Alt+ArrowDown` Move the selected items up or down; `Mod+D` Duplicate; `ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown` Next or previous; `ArrowRight`/`Space`/`PageDown`/`Enter` Next stop (presenting); `ArrowLeft`/`PageUp`/`Backspace` Previous stop (presenting); `Home`/`End` First or last; `Escape` Stop presenting; `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`Shift+ArrowLeft`/`Shift+ArrowRight`/`Shift+ArrowUp`/`Shift+ArrowDown` Nudge selected shapes; `Delete`/`Backspace` Delete; `Escape` Close or deselect.
