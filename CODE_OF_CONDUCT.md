# Code of conduct

This project keeps the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in short here; the full text is the one that holds.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project and its community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act and speak in ways
that make for an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## What we expect

Empathy and kindness toward other people; respect for opinions, points of view and experiences that differ
from yours; giving and gracefully taking constructive feedback; owning our mistakes, apologising to those
they affect, and learning from them; putting what is best for the whole community first.

## What we do not accept

Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind; trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks; harassment in public or in private; publishing
someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission;
anything else that could reasonably be thought inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in every space of the project (issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews) and wherever
someone represents it in public. Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@tinylittleoffice.com**; every
report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected. The maintainers may
correct, warn, suspend or ban, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, a permanent ban.
